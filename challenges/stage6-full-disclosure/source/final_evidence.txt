BLACKLINE CORP
INCIDENT RESPONSE - FINAL EVIDENCE REPORT

Case: Blackline Corp Breach
Classification: Internal Investigation Record

The incident-response investigation has confirmed that the compromise
of the Blackline Corp employee portal was not an isolated event.

Evidence recovered throughout the investigation established the
following sequence:

1. Publicly exposed information provided the attacker with useful
   reconnaissance information.

2. Weak protection of internal information revealed additional
   credential-related clues.

3. Hidden information embedded inside an image exposed another
   component required by the attacker.

4. The Blackline Corp employee portal contained an authentication
   weakness that allowed unauthorized access.

5. Server and authentication logs confirmed suspicious activity after
   the compromise and revealed the attacker's subsequent actions.

The evidence gathered across the investigation has now been correlated.

FINAL STATUS:
The intrusion path has been reconstructed and the incident is considered
resolved for the purposes of this investigation.
